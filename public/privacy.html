Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SkyVector Air Command collects minimal information necessary for the game's online features, such as your chosen username and game scores for the leaderboard.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used solely to display your rank on the global leaderboard and to facilitate multiplayer sessions via room codes.

3. Data Storage

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. Leaderboard data and multiplayer room states are stored securely on our Supabase backend.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
